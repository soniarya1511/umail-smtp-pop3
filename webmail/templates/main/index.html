How UMAIL Works – A Smarter Email System

UMAIL is more than just an email client—it’s an advanced Mail User Agent (MUA) that directly communicates with your SMTP and POP3 servers via secure socket connections.

SMTP Support

Send emails via SwiftMail & PostHawk servers.

POP3 Access

Reverse DNS & MX Records

Ensures reliable email delivery and authentication.

Connect, Send, and Receive with UMAIL

UMAIL integrates seamlessly with mail.swiftmail.com and mail.posthawk.com for reliable email communication. The system operates using three Docker containers, ensuring isolation, security, and smooth performance.
🔹 Your Emails, Your Control
📨 Send and receive emails securely using our MUA-Django web client.
🔗 Communicate directly with your email servers via custom socket connections.
🔧 Built with reverse DNS and MX records, ensuring proper email routing & authentication.

Built for Developers & Email Enthusiasts

Unlike traditional email clients, UMAIL is engineered for those who want full control over their email communication.
Tech Stack & Architecture
🔹 Mail Servers: SwiftMail & PostHawk (Docker-based SMTP & POP3)
🔹 DNS Server: Custom dnsmasq setup for MX & reverse DNS
🔹 Communication: Direct socket-based interaction between servers and UMAIL
🔹 Frontend: Built with Django for a smooth webmail experience

Why Choose UMAIL?

🔐 Secure & Private – Your emails are handled within a self-hosted, containerized environment.
⚡ Optimized Performance – With reverse DNS and MX records, email authentication and routing are seamless.
